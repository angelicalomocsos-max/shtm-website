Department Introduction

What is SHTM all About?

The School of Hospitality and Tourism Management (SHTM) is committed to developing top-notch, education-driven students to become globally competitive and exceptional life-long learners.

Vision

To become the top School of Hospitality and Tourism Management in the province which trains and deploys world-class professionals.

Mission

The School of Hospitality and Tourism Management (SHTM) is committed to developing top-notch, education-driven students to become globally competitive and exceptional life-long learners.

Our Commitment

Goals and Objectives

01

To constantly review, change, and improve the curriculum that is outcomes-based education to ensure the effectiveness of the college's program in preparing the students for successful careers.

02

To maintain a competent, qualified faculty that possesses current technical and professional knowledge and experience, engages in ongoing professional development and can convey this knowledge to students.

03

To develop the students' written and verbal skills as well as analytical and technical skills prior to graduation.

04

To provide facilities that support educational programs and enable students to develop profession-specific skills.

05

To engage and maintain a staff that takes care of the students and meets their educational goals and objectives.

06

To attract qualified students of diverse backgrounds.

07

To provide a collegiate academic atmosphere that encourages open exchange of ideas.

08

To engage faculty and students in research that serves to improve teaching and learning.

09

To develop programs and activities that provide community service aligned with the college's mission.

10

To submit the college to accreditation and assessment to see if it meets the minimum standards set by CHED and other accrediting agencies.

Our Story

Our Journey

From a humble beginning to a beacon of excellence in education.

2009

Founding Year

Southland College was established in Kabankalan City with a vision to provide top-tier education in the region.

2012

First Graduating Class

Celebrated the first batch of graduates, who went on to excel in their respective fields.

2015

Launch of Engineering Program

The School of Engineering was inaugurated, offering cutting-edge programs in technology and innovation.

2020

Campus Expansion

Completed the construction of a new wing for Arts & Sciences, providing modern facilities for students.

2024

A Decade of Excellence

Marked over 15 years of academic excellence and commitment to community development.
