About Our Program

Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management (BSHM) is a four-year degree program that covers the process of conception, planning, development, human resource and management of the different aspects of hotel, restaurant, and resort operations. The program provides students with technical skills, as well as knowledge in marketing, finance, budgeting, staffing and other fields of business. The program also aims to enhance entrepreneurship skills.

VISION

The Hospitality Management program is dedicated to imparting quality higher education which develops students to become experienced and proficient life-long professionals with outstanding managerial skills to compete globally.

MISSION

The BSHM program is committed to develop, transform and produce globally competent leaders in the hospitality industry through the use of comprehensive learning resources, state-of-the-art facilities and with the guidance of proficient instruction.
