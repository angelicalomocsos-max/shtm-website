What Our Students Say

Responses from our student survey on why they chose Hospitality and Tourism.

Why did you choose this course?

  • This is my dream course ever since.
  • Because it has a lot of opportunities and also so I can help my mother.
  • I want to explore more about the different cultures, traditions, beliefs, and most especially the hidden or lesser known places that we later discover and turn into tourist destinations.
  • I chose Tourism because I love traveling, exploring new places, and learning about different cultures.

What inspired you to study Hospitality or Tourism?

  • My passion for traveling and creating memorable experiences.
  • I'm curious about this course.
  • To explore and gain knowledge about the different types of people I'll meet in the future, their cultures, and different environments.
  • I was inspired by my interest in traveling and my dream of working in the tourism industry.

What do you know about your chosen course?

  • It's all about exploring a broad field that combines cultural education and hands-on service.
  • About how flexible it is.
  • It teaches me about traveling, different cultures, and beautiful destinations, and helps me develop communication, leadership, and customer service skills.
  • Tourism teaches us about travel, destinations, culture, tour guiding, and how to provide good service to tourists.

What are your career goals after graduation?

  • To gain experience in the industry and advance into leadership roles.
  • To take TESDA and then work abroad in the UAE with my mom and sister.
  • To become a flight attendant in the future.
  • To become a flight attendant or work in a well-known tourism company.
